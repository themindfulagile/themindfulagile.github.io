How we use your data at this event

This notice explains what personal data we collect when you register for and take part in a Mindful Agile webinar, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and Portuguese Law 58/2019.

Applies to: The Conformity Tax · [Date] and later sessions in the series · Last updated [Date]

In short

  • We only collect what we need to run the webinar and send you the follow-up materials.
  • Poll answers are anonymous. Nothing you say or type is used to evaluate you as an individual.
  • We never sell your data or pass it to anyone for their own marketing.
  • We only send newsletters or invitations to future events if you opted in. You can opt out at any time.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data whenever you like.

A note on the interactive exercises

Some exercises show how group dynamics shape the answers people give. Parts of an exercise may be set up in advance by the host team. When they are, we explain this during the same session. Your answers are never scored, reported or linked to you by name, and taking part is always optional.
