Who This Is For

Your teams are capable.
The system is holding them back.

I work with organizations facing these challenges — and translate them into measurable business outcomes.


Slow & unpredictable delivery

Sprints miss commitments, releases slip, and leadership loses confidence in the team's ability to forecast. The root cause is rarely the people — it's the system they're working in.

Transformation that isn't sticking

You adopted Agile or SAFe but the practices are shallow. Teams go through the motions without the mindset shift. The framework is there — the culture isn't.

Leadership not equipped for change

Leaders are being asked to manage differently — less command-and-control, more servant leadership — without the coaching or support to make that transition successfully.

High turnover & low engagement

Top performers leave when they don't feel heard, trusted, or developed. The cost of replacing one engineer is 1.5× their annual salary. Psychological safety is not a "nice to have."

Scaling without a playbook

The startup approach worked for 2 teams. Now there are 10 — and things are falling apart. Coordination, dependencies, and cross-team alignment become the new bottleneck.

Strategy not reaching the teams

Leadership has a clear strategy. The teams are doing something different. OKRs aren't connected to daily work, and the gap between vision and execution keeps widening.
